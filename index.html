FAQ

Questions data managers ask

Where does our data go?

With the free version, each form goes from your browser to OpenRouter and the AI provider you choose. Nothing is stored on FormLens servers. If your forms contain identifiable participant data, FormLens only routes requests to providers with zero data retention.

Which AI models can we use?

Any model on OpenRouter that can read images. FormLens recommends one based on your forms, privacy needs, priority and volume, and shows the estimated monthly cost.

How well does it handle handwriting?

It depends on the model and the form, which is why the pilot measures accuracy on your own pages. In every plan your team verifies each record before export.

Is it validated for regulated clinical trials?

Not yet. FormLens suits observational studies, surveillance and programme monitoring data today. A validation pack for GCP-regulated trials is planned.

Do we need to install anything?

No. FormLens runs in the browser. Profiles and sessions are saved as files you keep.
